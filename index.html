01 Museum data · Diversity

Closing the Gap: Met vs. MoMA

The Met has pledged to fill its Tang Wing, opening in 2030, with work by long-underrepresented artists. Benchmarking its Modern art acquisitions against MoMA, the expected gap did not appear: 17.1% of the Met's acquisitions are by non-Western artists, against 12.9% at MoMA, and in the 2010s the Met led the shift, 30.3% to 23.6%. Built from both museums' open collection data, with charts in the Met's own red and slate.

02 Machine learning · Music

Spotify Hit Predictor

Can a song's audio features alone predict a hit, with no artist fame or chart history? On 80,293 cleaned tracks (12% labeled hits), a Random Forest with SMOTE reached an F1 of 0.53 and nearly doubled recall, from 32% to 55%. Instrumentalness and acousticness outranked danceability as predictors. Along the way I caught a data leakage bug that had inflated cross-validation to 0.87, and fixed it with a pipeline.

03 A/B testing · Product

Vanguard Digital Redesign: A/B Test Analysis

Vanguard needed to know whether a redesigned interface with in-context prompts would get more clients to finish a key process without adding friction. Across a three-month experiment with 50,487 clients, the new design lifted completion from 49.6% to 58.4%, clearing Vanguard's 5-point threshold, and cut median completion time from 4.57 to 4.18 minutes. Drop-off peaked at the step 2 to step 3 transition. On this four-person team project, I handled the data cleaning, the hypothesis tests, and the deck for completion rate, median completion time, and time spent per step.

04 Capstone · Media trends

Page to Screen: Sci-Fi Books vs. Streaming

Is streaming sci-fi tracking what's being published, or running on its own agenda? This open-data stand-in for literary scouting compares about 14,700 books with 4,105 films and shows across 11 themes on one timeline. For themes like dystopia, cyberpunk, and space opera, screen commissioning led publishing by one to three years, and just 8.4% of commissioned titles were book adaptations. Findings are explored in an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
